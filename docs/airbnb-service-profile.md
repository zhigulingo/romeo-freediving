## Main service listing

### Title

Underwater photo and video shoot in Dahab

Alternative if Airbnb's title limit is shorter:

Underwater portraits in Dahab

### Short summary

A private underwater photo and video session in the Red Sea, guided by a certified freediving instructor. No freediving or modelling experience is required.

### Full description

Create an underwater portrait story in the clear waters of Dahab with personal guidance from preparation to the final frame.

We begin with a short conversation about your experience, comfort in the water and visual references. On location, I guide you through calm breathing, simple movement and safe underwater positioning. We then shoot at a depth and pace that feel comfortable for you—there is no target depth and no need to know how to pose.

Your session is private and adapted to the sea conditions, available light and your confidence in the water. Beginners can stay close to the surface; experienced freedivers can create more dynamic sequences. After the shoot, I select and professionally edit the agreed number of photographs and videos for private use and social media.

### What makes this service special

- The photographer is also a certified Molchanovs freediving instructor.
- Suitable for complete beginners and experienced freedivers.
- Personal breathing, movement and posing guidance.
- Private session at a pace and depth chosen for the guest.
- Professionally edited photographs and vertical social-ready video.
- Local knowledge of Dahab's Red Sea light and water conditions.

### Guest journey

1. **Plan the story** — We discuss experience, comfort, clothing and visual references before the session.
2. **Prepare on shore** — A short safety briefing, breathing practice and movement rehearsal.
3. **Shoot underwater** — A private guided session with pauses whenever needed.
4. **Receive the collection** — Roman selects and edits the agreed photographs and films and delivers them digitally.

### Who it is for

Adults who are comfortable entering the water. Prior freediving and modelling experience are not required. Guests should disclose relevant health conditions before the session and follow the instructor's safety directions at all times.

### Location

Dahab, South Sinai, Egypt. The exact shore-entry location is selected according to weather, sea conditions and the guest's experience, and is confirmed before the session.

### Languages

Russian and English — confirm the exact spoken-language level before publishing.
