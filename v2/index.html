04 · Before we dive

Ask me
anything.

The questions most people send before their first session.

I can’t hold my breath for long. Is that a problem?
Not at all

Ten to twenty relaxed seconds is enough for beautiful frames. Before the shoot, I’ll show you simple breathing and relaxation techniques.

How deep will we go?
Usually 1–10 metres

We choose the depth around your comfort and experience. Beginners often get their best images close to the surface, where the light is strongest.

Is it safe?
Safety comes first

I’m a certified Molchanovs instructor and active AIDA/CMAS athlete. I guide the full session and stay close throughout every dive.

When do I get the photos?
From 10 days

You receive a private cloud link with edited high-resolution photographs. Materials are sometimes ready sooner.
