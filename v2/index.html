What you’ll do

One morning.
Three chapters.

A clear route from dry land to your final dive, so you always know what comes next.

01

Meet, breathe, feel safe

We start with tea, references and a simple breathing session. You’ll learn how the shoot works and tell me what feels comfortable.

02

Find your movement

In shallow water we practise relaxed dives, soft hands and simple poses. No previous freediving or modelling experience is required.

03

Create the final frames

Once you forget the camera, we shoot. Romeo stays close, directs each sequence and builds the images around the light and your natural rhythm.
